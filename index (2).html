04 / Selected Work

Projects worth a closer look.

CAD and mechanism design work across SOLIDWORKS and Siemens NX — from mold tooling to functional assemblies.

01 — Mold Tooling

Injection Mould — Cavity & Core Design

Modeled a ribbed bowl-form part in SOLIDWORKS and built out the full mold tooling around it — parting line and parting surface, shut-off surfaces, and a tooling split to separate the core and cavity halves. Countersunk fastener holes and fillets were added for the finished mold block, with circular patterns used to keep the rib and hole features consistent.

SOLIDWORKSMold ToolsSurfacing
02 — Assembly & Motion

4-Jaw Lathe Chuck — Assembly & Motion Study

Built a functional 4-jaw chuck assembly — chuck body, scew fixer, lock pins, bolts, spindle screw, and jaws — as individually mated components rather than a single static model. Used SOLIDWORKS Motion Study with linear motors to simulate the jaws actuating symmetrically, verifying the mechanism moves and clears correctly before it would ever reach a machine.

SOLIDWORKSAssembly DesignMotion Simulation
03 — Surface Modeling

Turbine Impeller — Blade Surfacing

Modeled a multi-blade turbine impeller in Siemens NX, working from a revolved hub profile through swept and patterned blade surfaces. The curved blade geometry was unified into a single solid body and finished with a long sequence of edge blends across every blade root and tip to keep the aerodynamic surface clean and manufacturable.

Siemens NXSurface ModelingEdge Blending
04 — Integrated Component

Shaft-Turbine — Splined Drive Shaft

Extended the impeller work into a single integrated shaft-turbine component in Siemens NX — turbine blades on one end, transitioning through a revolved shaft body to a splined drive coupling on the other. Combined sketches, sweeps, and pattern features to keep the blade and spline geometry parametric on one continuous part.

Siemens NXShaft DesignSplines
05 — CAD / Mechanical Design

Pillar Drilling Machine — SOLIDWORKS CAD Design & Assembly

Designed and assembled a complete pillar drilling machine in SOLIDWORKS, from individual components through to the full assembly. Built as a parametric 3D model with accurate component detailing, standard mates, and an exploded structure to show how each part comes together — with a continued focus on precision, functionality, and a clean, industry-focused design approach.
